Refresh & notifications

Set all search filters directly on J.B. Hunt. This extension only refreshes those results and identifies new loads.

Each Play starts a silent baseline scan of every result page. After that, a load is new only when its ID has not appeared during this run. Price changes, reordered rows and returning loads do not trigger another notification.

New loads stay highlighted at the top of their page after refreshes and while paused. The New loads button keeps discoveries across pages, newest first, until the next Play or reload. Missing loads stay listed with their last-seen time; missing from results does not confirm booking. Every page request waits at least the selected interval; a complete search takes multiple intervals.

Keep Chrome and the load board open. Background tabs and sleeping computers can delay checks. Editing the site pauses monitoring; Play starts a new baseline. Booking stays manual.

Run a J.B. Hunt search, then press ▶ Play beside the pagination controls at the bottom. Monitoring starts paused after a reload.
